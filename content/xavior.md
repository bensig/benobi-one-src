## The Problem

Finding new customers means stitching together a lead list, an enrichment tool, a sending setup, and a sequencer — then babysitting all four.

Most of the time goes into setup, not conversations.

</div>

<div class="mp-section">

## What XAVIOR Does

Describe your ideal customer in plain English — "dentists in Texas," "CPAs," "software engineers." XAVIOR searches its AI-enriched lead database, enriches each profile, and writes hyper-targeted outreach.

New leads land in a review queue first. You approve, XAVIOR sends — paced, from inboxes on your own domain.

**Less setup. More conversations.**

</div>
